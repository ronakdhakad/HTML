About Apna Hotel

Apna Hotel was created with a simple idea — to offer the warmth of home with the comfort of a modern hotel. Over the years, we’ve hosted thousands of guests from across India and the world.

Our Story

What started as a small family-run guest house has grown into a full-service hotel, known for its personalised care and attention to detail. We believe hospitality is not just about rooms, but about how we make you feel during your stay.

From morning greetings at breakfast to helping you plan your day in the city, our team is always ready to assist you.

Our Promise

  • Clean and well-maintained rooms.
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden charges.
  • Helpful staff available around the clock.
  • Good food and a peaceful environment.
  • Continuous improvement based on guest feedback.
Apna Hotel — where every guest is treated like family.
